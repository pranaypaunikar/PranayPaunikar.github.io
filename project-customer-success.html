Customer Success
Health & Risk Framework.

A practical framework for turning customer relationship, service and operational signals into a structured view of customer health, risk and next actions.

Customer SuccessEnterprise CustomersRisk ManagementSynthetic example

THE IDEA

Move from scattered signals
to a clearer customer picture.

The problem

Customer health rarely comes from one metric. Service performance, incidents, escalations, engagement, unresolved concerns and business requirements can tell different parts of the story.

The approach

Combine a small set of repeatable signals into a health view, then use human judgment to understand the context behind the score and decide what action is appropriate.

What it demonstrates

  • Customer-centric thinking
  • Risk and escalation management
  • Service-performance interpretation
  • Structured customer reviews and follow-up

Design principle

A health score should start a conversation, not replace one. A customer marked “healthy” can still have an important relationship risk, while a temporary service issue does not automatically mean the relationship is unhealthy.

Example review questions

  • What changed since the last review?
  • Are any service issues creating relationship risk?
  • Which commitments remain open?
  • What does the customer need next?
  • Who owns the next action and by when?

Why it matters

Customer Success is strongest when relationship management and service operations inform each other. This framework helps create a common language for risks, priorities and follow-through without pretending that a single score can capture the whole relationship.

How AI can assist

With approved, non-sensitive inputs, AI can help summarize business-review notes, group recurring themes, draft follow-up actions and surface patterns across structured data. Final interpretation, customer communication and decisions remain human-owned.

This is a portfolio demonstration using synthetic information. It does not represent a real customer account, employer metric or confidential workflow.
